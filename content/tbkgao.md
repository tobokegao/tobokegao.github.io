{{< lang en >}}
TBKgao is an independent label focused on chiptune, started by Tobokegao in 2019 ("tobokegao's bootleg label", as its Bandcamp puts it).

All releases are on Bandcamp: [tbkgao.bandcamp.com](https://tbkgao.bandcamp.com/music)
{{< /lang >}}
