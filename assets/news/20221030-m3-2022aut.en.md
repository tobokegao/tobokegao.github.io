## Overview

- Date: October 30, 2022, from 10:30
- Place: Tokyo Ryutsu Center, Exhibition Hall 2, 1st floor (in person) and M3 Online (web)
- Booth: い-04b in person, 緑-007 on the web

M3 was the earliest place to get TOBOX. The price there was below the regular Bandcamp price ($18 plus shipping), with no shipping to pay and with visitors' travel costs and the hassle of making change in mind. Anyone who bought the CD-R at M3 and let Tobokegao know also got the Bandcamp digital files.

60 Million Reserve helped out at the booth.

## Looking back

Far more copies sold in person than expected, while the web event sold none. Even the stock set aside for Bandcamp ran out, so 100 more TOBOX CD-Rs were printed.

Talking with followers face to face was a real boost.
