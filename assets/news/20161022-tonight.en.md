## Looking back
The lineup was wide-ranging, and the event felt packed and fun. After the show, Tobokegao was moved to talk with many people, including the manga artist 風見2. In 2020, Tobokegao released "トゥナイト通信(Tonight)" on TBKgao, a track named after this event.
