## Lineup
- furmoph (LIVE): makes songs with a real Game Boy and Vocaloid. This was their first live show.
- マキシウキョウ (LIVE): drawing attention with songs using 重音テト SV. Also a first live show.
- サツキ (DJ): still based in Hokkaido even after "メズマライザー" passed 100 million views on YouTube. Their DJ sets strongly reflect their own taste.
- 真島ゆろ (DJ): known for songs written for P丸様。 and 学マス. Tobokegao keeps coming back to the bittersweet lyrics and pop songwriting.
- なみぐる (DJ): known for ずんだもん songs, and DJs while playing the saxophone.
- 長谷川迷子 (DJ): organizer of the "ウソのつきかた" compilation series, on the lineup for the third time, planning a remix-focused set.
- Tobokegao (door, VJ)

## Looking back
The start ran a little late, and because of a problem 真島ゆろ's set moved to after マキシウキョウ. Tobokegao focused on the door, and also tried a VJ set that paired each performer with a YouTube video. Tobokegao's "黄泉で足踏み" (重音テト ver) was also played that night, which Tobokegao was very grateful for.
