## About the making
A track made by playing around with Game Boy sound effects. Tobokegao submitted the track with its LSDj file name; after the file extension was dropped, "SKWEEEEE B4" simply became the final track title. The performance video was re-recorded, so the way the tones change differs a little from the released audio.
