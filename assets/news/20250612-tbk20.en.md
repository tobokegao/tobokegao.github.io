## Looking back
There was no stream, but Tobokegao published the otoMAD-mix as a video at the same moment as the set, so people who couldn't come could feel the mood. It was an unusually high-energy, club-ready set for Tobokegao. Afterwards Tobokegao shared the list of otoMADs played between sets, and wrote a note article about how the mix was made.
