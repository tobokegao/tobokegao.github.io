## Overview

- Date: August 30, 2020
- Place: the Micromusic Italy stream on Twitch
- Tobokegao's part: a 25-minute mix of 13 8-bit girl-pop covers (plus 3 more), made on Game Boy with LSDj. All were works in progress.

## Looking back

The set was posted on SoundCloud and YouTube as "Gameboy LSDj 8bit GiRLPOP Cover WIP Collection", with the setlist on YouTube. Tobokegao also made a playlist of the original songs.

The WIP versions of "Stop" and "Skip" (LSDj 8.8.2) played that day were added to the TBKgao Bandcamp as well.
