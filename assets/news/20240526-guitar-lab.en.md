## Overview

- Date: May 26, 2024, 18:00 to 21:00
- Venue: the lodge at Sapporo Art Park
- Organizer: Tobokegao
- Admission: free
- Tobokegao's part: hosted the meetup and tested the amps everyone brought.

"ギター音出し研究会" (a guitar sound study group) is a gathering for taking apart how guitars, effects and amps behave, and for finding unexpected tricks. It began online in April 2024, and this was its first meetup in person. People brought several unusual Japanese solid-state amps to hear how each one sounds.
