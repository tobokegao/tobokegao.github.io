## About the making
It began as a remix of a Klirre track. Layering guitar-like sounds over the original, a four-on-the-floor electro-style song, the original faded away and it became an original song, so it is credited as a feature. It was made with 2xLSDj, which links two Game Boys, and one of them was filled entirely by recreating four guitar strings. It was Tobokegao's first track to combine several odd time signatures.

Klirre asked for a Japanese title naming a turbojet, so Tobokegao chose the Junkers Jumo 004, a German-built jet engine, in connection with where Klirre is from. "004" is the engine's model number.
