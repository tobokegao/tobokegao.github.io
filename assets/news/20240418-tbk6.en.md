## Looking back
ハチナツ, first announced for this night, could not make it; after that, 長谷川迷子 joined. There was no stream this time, and works with crude jokes were played with the sound and picture edited. By that evening it looked like more people were coming than to any of the five earlier nights. Tobokegao's set was sprinkled with YTPMVs made from overseas sources Tobokegao did not know, and Tobokegao was surprised to have managed to build 45 minutes of it.
