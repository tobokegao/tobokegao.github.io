## Overview
- Game: YARS RISING (Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4/5, Xbox One / Series X|S, Steam and Epic Games Store)
- Release date: Tuesday, September 10, 2024
- Developer: WayForward
- Publisher: Atari
- Song: Nouveau Monde
- Tobokegao's part: Vocals, vocal melody and lyrics
- Tommy Pedrini's part: Composition and arrangement of the backing track

## Soundtrack
The official soundtrack, "The Sound of Yars Rising", includes two versions, "Nouveau Monde (Reiwa)" and "Nouveau Monde (Heisei)", and Tobokegao sings on both. Tommy Pedrini composed and arranged the backing track, and Tobokegao wrote the vocal melody to fit it and sang it. The songs are credited to Atari Music, Tobokegao and Tommy Pedrini. The soundtrack came out on Steam on Friday, September 13, 2024, and later on streaming services.
