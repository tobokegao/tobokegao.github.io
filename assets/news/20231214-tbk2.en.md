## Overview
- Date: Thursday, December 14, 2023, 19:00–22:00
- Venue: PLASTIC THEATER, Sapporo
- Flyer: Tobokegao
- Theme: Pure MIDI (raw MIDI sound). The theme was first announced as "old MIDI sound modules" and then renamed.

## Lineup
- Tobokegao (DJ): moved from MIDI house to Pure MIDI and then to midicore, a blend of MIDI and hardcore.
- 萌尽狼
- cocorocolony

## Looking back
Streaming started with this volume. There was a connection problem partway through, but the stream came back soon. Before the doors opened, Tobokegao remarked that the night, though billed as Pure MIDI, was starting to feel like a mixed martial arts match. Afterwards Tobokegao uploaded the DJ mix to SoundCloud and said that 萌尽狼's songs have historical value.
