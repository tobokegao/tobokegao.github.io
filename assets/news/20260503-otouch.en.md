## Overview
- Date: Sunday, May 3, 2026, 14:00–18:00
- Venue: FORESTLIMIT, Hatagaya
- Theme: Grab the video, touch the sound: a real-time otoMAD performance event born from touching input devices
- Admission: 1,800 yen (plus a 700 yen drink charge)
- Capacity: 60 people, staff included (reservations via twipla)
- Tobokegao's part: Organizer and otoMAD performance

## Looking back
Some acts looked set to make the visuals react to the venue and the audience, which would be hard to follow from afar, and routing each performer's computer to a stream would be complicated, so the event was not streamed. Reservations closed exactly at capacity, and a few more places opened up when cancellations came in about a week before the event. The performances included video that reacts to sound, video synced to live playing, source clips turned into a step sequencer, game development, and lights and even a drone moving with the sound. Tobokegao made an otoMAD on the spot by scratching and glitching, and later posted a video recreating it.
