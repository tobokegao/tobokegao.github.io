## Overview
- Release date: Friday, April 29, 2022
- Title: hey, ily! "Psychokinetic Love Songs"
- Label: Lonely Ghost Records
- Track: 1. "Rebooting"
- Tobokegao's part: Spoken word (in Japanese)

## About the making
Tobokegao joined the first track of the chip rock band hey, ily!'s album with a radio-style spoken part in Japanese, recorded between summer and autumn. The band gave no direction, and said the first take Tobokegao sent was what made the track.

## Looking back
At the time of recording, Tobokegao did not know that "ily" stands for "I love you," and the take where it was pronounced as "Ily" (イリー) ended up being used.
