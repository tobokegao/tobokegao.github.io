## Overview

- Release date: June 28, 2024 (announced by TBKgao on June 29)

## About the release

The setting is a futuristic city built within a megatall skyscraper. Each track on the album stands for a different floor and its surroundings. This song starts the musical journey to the summit.

The album "Arcology" [TBK-037] came out on May 2, 2025.
