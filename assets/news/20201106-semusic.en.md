## Overview

- Release date: November 6, 2020
- Format: Bandcamp, Apple Music
- Label: TBKgao [TBK-008]

Tobokegao's first EP and the eighth release on TBKgao: an EP about making as many songs as possible out of sound effects (SE).

## Tracks

1. Telephone Funk Octravia (Game Loop Version)
2. Skweeeee B4 (B5 version)
3. Dogsled Race Menu Theme

## Credits

- Cover: Competor (Kuchiwo Tsugumi)

## About the making

Because the songs are built from sound effects, Tobokegao would rather call them "SE Music" than chiptune, and that became the title.

The EP includes the song data (.sav), in which kicks and many other sounds were rebuilt and tried out. Try running it in LSDj 8.9.3. Lately the sounds seem less likely to break when LSDj's behavior changes, so the data should be easy to learn from.
