Tobokegao introduced it as "CHIPTUNE FREE JAZZ NOISE!!!", explaining that LSDj, the Game Boy music software, is glitched using the Python programming language and left to play.

Tracks 1, 2, 4 and 5 were first released as part of Weekly Beats 2016.
