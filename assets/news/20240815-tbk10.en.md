## Looking back
The typhoon missed the area, and hosotake and 佐藤あんこ came all the way from outside Hokkaido. ムメモ opened, followed by Tobokegao, ossa and hosotake, with 佐藤あんこ closing, and the night ended safely. Even within girl pop, each DJ's picks showed their own taste, which made it a fun night. Tobokegao's setlist was shared later as an image.
