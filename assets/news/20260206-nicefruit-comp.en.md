## Overview

- Release date: February 6, 2026
- Format: Bandcamp
- Label: TBKgao [TBK-040]

A compilation in which each artist rearranged a song from MellMell's album "すてきな果実 (Nice fruit)" with MIDI sounds and added synthesized vocals. Songs first made with a band sound are rebuilt by twelve acts. Tobokegao took part with track 5, "才能".
