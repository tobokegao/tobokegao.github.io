## About the making
Tobokegao made the remix with Game Boy music software, as a challenge to see how much wistfulness the Game Boy's simple sounds could carry. Tobokegao also suggested it might sound nice through a phone speaker. In January 2017, Tobokegao posted a tip from this track: layering noise over a light kick makes a strong snare.
