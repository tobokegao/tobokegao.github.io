## Overview
- Title: HATSUNE MIKU Digital Stars 2025 Compilation
- Release date: Friday, September 5, 2025
- Distributor: KARENT (Crypton Future Media)
- Format: CD (a standard edition and a limited edition with a two-layer acrylic board)
- Track: 4. "Radiant Revival (Tobokegao Remix)" (Jamie Paige feat. Hatsune Miku)

Tobokegao announced the contribution on July 10, 2025, when the tracklist was revealed.

## About the making
A remix of "Radiant Revival", the Digital Stars 2025 theme song, made in M8 Tracker with a Y2K feel. Tobokegao had been listening to Jamie Paige since before they became a Vocaloid producer, having discovered the music through Digital Fusion.
