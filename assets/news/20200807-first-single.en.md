## About the making

Pre-orders opened while the songs were still unfinished, and the work in progress was updated every week until they were done. Tracks 3 to 9 are those in-progress versions. The pre-order page said: "If you buy them, my pleasure will be reflected in the tracks and the quality may be improved."

The two tracks were completed on September 4, 2020. "Skip" later appeared on the album "Room To Room" [TBK-009] in an album version.
