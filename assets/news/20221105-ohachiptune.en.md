## Overview

- Release date: October 25, 2022 (announced by TBKgao on November 5)
- Format: Bandcamp
- Label: TBKgao [TBK-021]

The first album by Rei8bit. It grew out of the greeting "Oha Chiptune!" ("Good morning, chiptune!") and collects twenty bright, light chiptunes on the theme of morning.

## About the release

Every track was made in FamiStudio. The sound chip differs from track to track.

- 2A03: tracks 2, 3, 6 and 19
- MMC5: tracks 7 and 14
- VRC6: tracks 4, 5, 11, 15, 16 and 17
- FDS: track 9
- Sunsoft 5B: track 10
- N163: tracks 8 and 20
- VRC7: tracks 1, 13 and 18

The Bandcamp page also offers the song data (.nsf).

Tobokegao introduced the album by saying that its highly technical use of the NES sound chips shows how much chiptune music Rei8bit has listened to, absorbed and made their own. The second album, "Late Night 2-hours Chiptune Vol.1" [TBK-022], came out on the same day.
