## Looking back
From this volume on, the new-release showcase became part of Tobokegao's DJ time, switching seamlessly with the regular set. Rei8bit sent a new track, "Frosty Dusk", that same afternoon, and the night opened with it. Tobokegao summed it up as three kinds of square waves, one from each performer, and posted the DJ mix and setlist the next day.
