## Looking back
In the run-up, Tobokegao started making one song a day on the M8 Tracker and posting a video of each, and released "M8 FEST COMPILATION" (TBK-032) on TBKgao, a compilation of songs by the performers. On the day, the audio was streamed on Twitch, and the archive stayed up afterwards. The first half of Tobokegao's set was a playful exploration of FM-synth patches. Afterwards, VJ おしりマグナム took an interest in the headless version of the M8 Tracker, which felt like one of the event's goals, "winning new M8 Tracker users," had come true.
