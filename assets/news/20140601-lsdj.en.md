## Overview
- Date: Sunday, June 1, 2014
- Tobokegao started making music with LSDj (Little Sound DJ), music software for the Game Boy.
- This marks the start of Tobokegao's activity.

## How it began
At the time, things had become difficult between the members of the band Tobokegao was in. To heal from that, Tobokegao began making music in LSDj, looking back fondly on childhood days spent playing the Game Boy, remembered as the happiest time.
