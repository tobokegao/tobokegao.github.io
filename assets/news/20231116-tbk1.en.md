## Overview
- Date: Thursday, November 16, 2023, 19:00–22:00
- Venue: PLASTIC THEATER, Sapporo
- Theme: Chiptune
- Flyer: Tobokegao
- The first night of a monthly event with a themed DJ set and a new-release showcase. The DJ sets followed the theme; the showcase was open to any genre.

## Lineup
- Tobokegao (DJ): announced a set of dance-leaning chiptune and Digital Fusion.
- scythe (さいず) (DJ): also shared a new track in the showcase.

## Looking back
In the showcase, the audience got to hear a new track by さいず. Before the doors opened, Tobokegao had announced a plan to play a new track by Rei8bit and then share Tobokegao's own finds from SoundCloud. A stream had been planned, but there was none that night. Afterwards, Tobokegao posted the setlist as a SoundCloud playlist and uploaded a DJ mix. Later it turned out the flyer and hashtag had the name misspelled; the correct name is "To Be Kontinued".
