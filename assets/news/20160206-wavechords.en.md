## Overview
- Release date: Saturday, February 6, 2016
- Label: ESC TRAX (ESC-002)
- Release: First EP "Wave Chords EP" (3 tracks, digital)
- Cover: fm

## Tracks
1. ブルーベリーパイ [blueberry pie]
2. 空色平原(100年前) [azure fields(100 years ago)]
3. サンドバック [sandbag]

## About the making
The theme was "band-like songs using pseudo-chords on the wave channel." The LSDj song data (lsdsng) for tracks 2 and 3 was shared too. With "Blueberry Pie," Tobokegao wanted a lo-fi, band-like song, and managed to blend in every sound Tobokegao wanted. All three tracks were later included on "Picnic (Deluxe Edition)."

## Interview
The day after the release, on Sunday, February 7, 2016, the ESC TRAX blog published an interview with Tobokegao. It covers how Tobokegao found chiptune, what Tobokegao pays attention to when making music, and the highlights of this EP.
