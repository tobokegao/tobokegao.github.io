## Overview
- Date: Monday, August 10, 2020 (the day the piece was announced)
- Zine: "ゆうがたのくに" issue 14 (made by the creator of ニセトボケガオ)
- Tobokegao's part: A piece built around the theme "sounds to listen to when a ghost might show up"

## Content
The piece is about what Tobokegao looks for in music, with reviews and notes on girl-pop songs for such moments. The TBKgao icon also appeared in the ad section of the same issue.

## Looking back
Writing it made Tobokegao realize that, in daily life, Tobokegao may keep the kind of distance from people that one keeps from a ghost.
