## Overview
- Published: Wednesday, November 16, 2016
- Article: Bandcamp Daily, "The New Faces of Japanese Chiptune" (Scene Report)
- Writer: Patrick St. Michel
- Featured artists: Toriena, NNNNNNNN, Breezesquad, Professor Sakamoto, Chibi-Tech, Maru303, Gigandect, Foilverb, Tobokegao and more

## About the article
The article introduces Tobokegao, from Sapporo, as an artist whose bright little melodies hide painstaking detail. It describes how Tobokegao packs countless small flourishes, and even animal sounds, into songs made with Little Sound DJ (LSDj), and points to "Dogsled Race Replay Theme" and "Sherbet" from that year's album "Picnic" as examples.

## Afterwards
In June 2020, Tobokegao shared on X that the "Picnic" player in the article had switched on its own to the version re-released on TBKgao.
