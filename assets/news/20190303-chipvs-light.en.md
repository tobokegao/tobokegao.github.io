## Overview
- Release date: Sunday, March 3, 2019
- Organizer: しのりゅー (Sinoryu, novtos)
- Title: 光のチップチューンVS闇のチップチューン 対決コンピレーション -Light Side- (Light Chiptune VS Dark Chiptune Battle Compilation, 23 tracks, hashtag #ChipVS_Light_Dark)
- Format: Digital (Bandcamp)
- Tobokegao's part: Track 18, "Sunshine, Sunrise, Sunset" (Commodore 64 style, Fake SID)

## About the making
The plan was a song about gazing absently at sunlight, but it became a wild, fuzz-like track limited to the three channels of the Commodore 64. The title is what remains of words meant as lyrics for the intro.

## Afterwards
At Square Sounds Tokyo 2019 in September 2019, 2PColor reportedly performed this track live with added vocals. A new 2PColor release also includes a bonus track sung over it.
