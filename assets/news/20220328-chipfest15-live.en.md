## Overview

- Release date: March 28, 2022
- Format: Bandcamp
- Label: TBKgao [TBK-014]

Tobokegao's first live album, recorded at the streamed event Chipfest Cyberspace 15 on March 26, 2022. All ten songs are vocal arrangements.

## Tracks

1. Blueberry Pie (Vocal Version)
2. Develop (Vocal Version)
3. Kodoku Na Fruits (Vocal Version)
4. Shampoo (Vocal Version)
5. Skip (Vocal Version)
6. To Quit (Vocal Version)
7. Yomi De Ashibumi (Vocal Version)
8. Dear Ghost (Vocal Version)
9. Tired Face (The Game Never Ends) (Vocal Version)
10. Why (Vocal Version)

## About the making

Chipfest Cyberspace 15 was a streamed event presented by calmdownkidder, starting at 7pm UK time on March 26. The lineup was Danimal Cannon, exciting!!excellent!!, itspandaonair, Jellica and Tobokegao. The set was played with LSDj and M8 Tracker. A recording of the stream is on YouTube.
