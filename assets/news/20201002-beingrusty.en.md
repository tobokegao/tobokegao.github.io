## Overview

- Release date: October 2, 2020
- Format: Bandcamp, Apple Music
- Label: TBKgao [TBK-007]

Tobokegao's second single, a collaboration with migmint. The music video is on YouTube and on niconico (loop version).

1. Being Rusty (Tanashii Mainichi)
2. Being Rusty (Tanashii Mainichi) (Vocal & Guitar Version) feat. AntiChiptuneChiptuneClub
3. Being Rusty (Tanashii Mainichi) GB inst only
4. Being Rusty (Tanashii Mainichi) guiter only
5. Being Rusty (Tanashii Mainichi) a cappella
6. Being Rusty (Demo)

## Credits

- Lyrics and music: Tobokegao
- Animation and artwork: migmint

## About the making

migmint's videos and lyrics inspired a chippy song. Once it was written, Tobokegao collaborated with the very person who had inspired it. It is a short looping background track, made while picturing an animation of someone absorbed in a game alone.

## Looking back

A week after release, a demo from when the vocal still had chipcrusher on it was added as track 6, "Being Rusty (Demo)".

The song later appeared on the album "TOBOX" [TBK-019] in a new recording.
