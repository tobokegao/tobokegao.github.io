## Looking back
Many YTPMVs are built around chiptune and Keygen Music tracks, so the set centered on music tied to that scene. Tobokegao kept refining the setlist right up until start time, ending up with a mix that was half YTPMV-style tracks and half Tobokegao's own favorites.
