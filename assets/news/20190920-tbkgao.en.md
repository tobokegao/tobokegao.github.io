## Overview

- Founded: September 20, 2019
- TBKgao is Tobokegao's own label.
- First release: [TBK-001] Bendelic Freaks "Circuit Bending GB" (CD, digital and streaming; free on the Internet Archive)

## Credits (TBK-001)

- Artwork: PlhectorMusic (drawn in Flipnote Studio on the Nintendo DS)
- Design and more: Tobokegao

## About the making

"Circuit Bending GB", released together with the label's launch, is a noise album made on circuit-bent Game Boys. The CD artwork is printed on the disc itself too, and spinning it in a player where the disc is visible makes the pixel art animate. The noise from exporting out of Flipnote Studio gives it a lo-fi flavor.
