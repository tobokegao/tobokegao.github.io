## About the making

PURE MIDI is a genre Tobokegao made up: music played on sound modules with little or no effects. In Tobokegao's words, it feels like "listening to someone's song left inside second-hand hardware". Deeply influenced by MLTEK's music, Tobokegao wondered whether a compilation of such songs could be made, and this album is the result.

The entries split into those that lean into a thin, "pena-pena" sound and those that push technique as far as it goes within the limits, so the first half holds the former and the second half the latter. The gear used was kept secret on purpose, so that listeners focus on how the music sounds.

## Looking back

After the announcement, Tobokegao expected little response to something this niche, and was glad it caught on more than expected. The night before release, the whole album premiered on YouTube while listeners posted their reactions together.
