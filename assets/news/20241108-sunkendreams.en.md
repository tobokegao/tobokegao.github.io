## Overview

- Release date: November 8, 2024
- Format: Bandcamp

## About the release

After a catastrophic natural disaster, the remaining civilians are relocated to a megatall skyscraper. To many of them this symbol of prosperity stands for their hopes of a new life, but they soon uncover the bitter truth as dreams collide with reality.

The song later became track 3 of the album "Arcology" [TBK-037].
