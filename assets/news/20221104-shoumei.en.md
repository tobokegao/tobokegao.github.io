## Overview

- Release date: November 4, 2022
- Format: Bandcamp
- Label: TBKgao [TBK-020]

The second album by Yagishiro, titled "証明" ("Proof"). Chippy sounds meet the soft, fluffy voice of Hatsune Miku, and every song has something lovable about it.

2. ユウレイタウン

4. tanuki
5. nekomimi
6. evidence
7. contomin

9. ウソの文明
10. 山びこの日
11. Floating Elephant
