## Overview
- Release date: Wednesday, May 4, 2016
- Label: Cheapbeats (CB056)
- Release: First mini album "Picnic" (7 tracks, digital and CD; the Bandcamp version comes with LSDj save data for every song)
- Artwork: castpixel
- Mastering: Toni Leys

## Tracks
1. ピクニック (Picnic)
2. バースデーパーティ (Birthday Party)
3. ブルーベリーパイ (Blueberry Pie)
4. イヌぞりレース リプレイのテーマ (Dogsled Race Replay Theme)
5. シャーベット (Sherbet)
6. つかれた顔(ゲームは終わらない) (Tired Face (The Game Never Ends))
7. なぜ (Why?)

## About the making
Seven songs that pack in two years of work. The goal was chiptune to listen to on the way home after a day out, eating, drinking and getting everything done. Tobokegao asked castpixel for artwork on the theme of a character bento, and the food and design were all castpixel's ideas. The three songs shared before the release were also reworked for the album.

## Afterwards
In 2020, the album was re-released on Tobokegao's own label, TBKgao, followed by "Picnic (Deluxe Edition)" the same year.
