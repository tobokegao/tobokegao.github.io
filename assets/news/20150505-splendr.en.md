## Overview
- Released: Tuesday, May 5, 2015
- Release: Electric Yakuza's single "魔法の戦闘少女: Splendr" (Mesmerize Records)
- Written by: Kid MarsCat
- Track: 5. "Splendr (Bitpop Remix)"

## Afterwards
On May 7, 2015, Tobokegao also shared the remix on SoundCloud as "Electric Yakuza - Splendr [GB Bitpop Remix]." On May 1, 2020, a remastered version, "Splendr (tobokegao Bitpop Remix) [Remaster]," was included on TBKgao's "Collab Works 01."
