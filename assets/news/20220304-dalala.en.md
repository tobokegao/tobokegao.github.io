## About the making

A laid-back scat song made in M8 Tracker. It grew out of "DA-LA-LA" posted to WeeklyBeats, though the arrangement changed completely. Tobokegao describes it as a blend of favorite parts: the intro synth of "Blow up", the brass of "しようよ", the bass of "あの娘が眠ってる" and the shouts of "Beeper Love", joined to an arrangement of one of Tobokegao's own songs. Listening to those influences is an easy way into Tobokegao's taste in music.
