## Overview
- Date: Sunday, December 18, 2016, 14:00–20:00
- Venue: 音楽喫茶 茶箱 (SABACO), Waseda
- Admission: 2,000 yen (one drink included)
- Organizers: sdhizumi and kc (co-organized)
- Event: Yuru-Tune-Lounge, a one-time, relaxed chiptune event
- Lineup: Nishioka Diddley, 赤帯, Robotprins, 梅干茶漬け, sdhizumi, ウラボロシ, suesett, 谷本早也歌, Vince Kaichan, Tobokegao
- Tobokegao's part: Live set (17:45–18:15, played on a GBASP)

## Looking back
Tobokegao was on the lineup but enjoyed the day just as much as a listener. Looking back, Tobokegao said it may have been the first event they had been to with such a gentle mood, where square waves did not battle each other.
