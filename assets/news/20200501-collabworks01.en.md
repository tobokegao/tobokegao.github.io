## Overview

- Release date: May 1, 2020 (CD-R from June 5)
- Format: Bandcamp, CD-R
- Label: TBKgao [TBK-003]

Tobokegao's first collaboration album, bringing together works by thirteen musicians and a Game Boy from 2014 to 2020.
