## Looking back
Having been deeply moved as a listener at an earlier edition, Tobokegao joined as a DJ this time. The mix gathered everything from GiRLPOP, which Tobokegao got into three years earlier, to recently loved bishōjo game songs, and took a lot of reworking to fit about two hours of tracks Tobokegao wanted to play into 45 minutes. So many songs didn't make it that Tobokegao wanted to do it again even before the day.
