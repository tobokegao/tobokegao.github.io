## Overview
- Date: Sunday, November 17, 2024, 18:30–21:30
- Venue: Terminal Plaza Kotoni Pathos, Gallery A, Sapporo
- Organizer: ギター音出し研究会
- Theme: A bazaar for buying and selling guitar effects pedals
- Admission: Free
- Tobokegao's part: Organizer

## Vendors
- ミライノオンガク: inada's PA and sound business, with a booth for making IR reverbs and a display of inada's pedals.
- クロイトーンオーディオ: A new music shop in Shiroishi Ward, showing three new fuzz pedals by クロイ.

## Looking back
Tobokegao brought an amp for trying out pedals, and asked visitors to bring their own instruments. With so much gear to carry, load-in and setup started well before opening. The bazaar ended safely, and Tobokegao thanked everyone who came and helped pack up, hoping to hold it again around next spring.
