## About the making

Going back to basics, every track was composed in LSDj v4.7.3. "Develop" and "J-POP Strings Pulse #1" appear in their finished versions, as does "Battle of the Bits Deluxe".

## Looking back

A few tracks were fixed after release. "シャーベット (Sherbet)" had unintended distortion in its second half and was replaced with a clean version. "ピクニック (Picnic)" was fixed for over-compression, and the outro fade of "つかれた顔(ゲームは終わらない)" was made smoother.
