## Overview
- Date: Thursday, February 15, 2024, 19:00–22:00
- Venue: PLASTIC THEATER, Sapporo
- Theme: Y2K VGM Jungle (jungle and atmospheric drum and bass with a PlayStation-era feel)
- Admission: 2,000 yen in advance / 2,500 yen at the door (+1 drink)
- Flyer: co-designed by mewgumi and Tobokegao

## Lineup
- 茄川 (DJ): one of the game music diggers Tobokegao respects, who also helped a lot with finding tracks for the Discord server "Y2K VGM". Joined with a DJ mix, as 茄川 could not be at the venue.
- Tobokegao (DJ): introduced as a set likely to be packed with Tobokegao's own favorites.
- WAN (DJ): invited because the EP "SUSUKINO JUNGLIST", made with a Polyend Tracker, seemed to fit the theme. Played records.
- Hizuo (DJ): invited because "Take A Bow" had a Y2K VGM feel.
- STAY GOLD MAN (DJ): invited out of interest in STAY GOLD MAN's work with Wiredflame, the event that inspired this one.
- isle (DJ): invited after a wonderful mix at Wiredflame.

## Looking back
With so many performers, there was no time for the new-release showcase this time. inaharu did not appear this time, but before the night Tobokegao said inaharu's tracks would be worked into Tobokegao's own set. The next day Tobokegao uploaded the DJ mix "Y2K VGM Vibes Jungle Mix" to SoundCloud.
