## Overview
- Release date: Friday, October 4, 2019 (on sale at Kaden no Ken-chan)
- Software: Caravan Boomer Caravan Edition! (an early edition of the MSX2 shooting game "Caravan Boomer")
- Developer: N.I & BO DSGN
- Format: 3.5-inch floppy disk in a small cardboard package
- Price: 1,600 yen (tax excluded) in-store at Kaden no Ken-chan; 1,200 yen by mail order (shipping extra)
- Tobokegao's part: One of the composers of the game's BGM (Tobokegao's track is "黄泉で足踏み", listed for stage 11 on the soundtrack)

## What it was
A promotional disk focused on time attack. It has two caravan modes, plus a boss and a title screen found only in this edition. A full version with the normal mode was planned for later.
