## Overview
- Early sale: Saturday, March 6, 2021 (Tokyo Game Music Show)
- General release: Saturday, March 13, 2021
- Format: One music CD with a two-page booklet; later also on streaming services
- Label: SweepRecord [SRIN-1177]
- Title: Caravan Boomer Soundtrack (music from "キャラバンブーマー", a shooting game for the MSX2)
- Tracks: 12. "黄泉で足踏み (Stage 11)" and 25. "黄泉で足踏み (Stage 11) Another Ver."

## About the making
The game's soundtrack is played using MSX-MUSIC (FM synthesis) and PSG. Tobokegao noted that "黄泉で足踏み" would never have come about without the MSX2's FM sound chip.
