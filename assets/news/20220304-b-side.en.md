## Overview
- Release date: Friday, March 4, 2022
- Format: Bandcamp (free download)
- Organizer: miles morkri (also mastered the album)
- Track: No. 52, "DA-LA-LA (B Vocal Version)"

## The prompt
On March 1, 2022, miles morkri posted a video with a prompt: add chords and instruments under a piano playing a B on quarter notes at 125 BPM. According to the organizer's announcement, 58 artists wrote music from this prompt, and the results became one compilation.

On the same day, March 4, the EP "DA-LA-LA (Vocal Version)" also came out on TBKgao.
