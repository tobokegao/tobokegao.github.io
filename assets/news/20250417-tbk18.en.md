## Overview
- Date: Thursday, April 17, 2025, 19:00–22:00
- Venue: PLASTIC THEATER, Sapporo
- Theme: Game Boy chiptune live (every performer played live on a Game Boy; chiptune, techno, hardcore)
- Admission: 2,000 yen in advance / 2,500 yen at the door
- Flyer: Tobokegao

## Lineup
- Saitone (LIVE): a pioneer of spacious electronica made with nanoloop since the early days of chiptune. The hardware version of nanoloop was also planned to appear.
- yukkerom (LIVE): back again after a ritual-like hardcore chiptune set last year.
- うみのひやけ (LIVE): plays technical wavetable basslines on a modded Game Boy, back again after last year.
- Tobokegao (LIVE): gabber kicks on the Game Boy, and some songs played together with guitar.
- MURASE Ryosuke (canceled)

## Looking back
MURASE Ryosuke had to cancel suddenly, so the timetable was rearranged on the day. うみのひやけ's wavetable movement and yukkerom's use of silence stood out. Saitone's knob work left an afterglow that lasted into the next day.
