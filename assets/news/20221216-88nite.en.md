## Looking back
The November 88nite had been so much fun that Tobokegao joined this time as a DJ. The set brought together game music Tobokegao had spent about half a year digging up on the Y2K MUSIC Discord server. On December 18, the set went up on YouTube as "20221216 88nite Tobokegao Y2K VGM MIX".
