## Overview
- Released: Thursday, May 11, 2017
- Project: Relay Bros (a group making relay medleys by many artists)
- Title: Blaster Master (a medley of music from the NES game "Blaster Master", known in Japan as 超惑星戦記メタファイト)
- Organizer: Dya
- Mastering: Toni Leys
- Artwork: Dex McKinnery
- Tobokegao's part: Joined as a new member, with a glitch hop part on Game Boy
