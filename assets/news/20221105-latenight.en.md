## About the release

In "Late Night 2-hours DTM", a theme is announced at 22:00 and participants have two hours, until 24:00, to make a piece of music on it. This album collects the tracks made between March 12 and June 4, 2022.

Every track was made in FamiStudio. The sound chip differs from track to track.

- 2A03: track 12
- MMC5: tracks 7 and 10
- VRC6: tracks 2, 6 and 11
- FDS: tracks 4 and 5
- N163: tracks 1 and 9
- VRC7: tracks 3, 8 and 14
- VRC6 and FDS: track 13

The Bandcamp page also offers the song data (.nsf). The first album, "Oha Chiptune!" [TBK-021], came out on the same day.
