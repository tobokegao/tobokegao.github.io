## Overview

- Release date: February 4, 2022
- Format: Bandcamp
- Label: TBKgao [TBK-012]

Tobokegao's second EP, "孤独なフルーツ (Vocal Version)" (Kodoku Na Fruits). The title track is a laid-back FM power-pop song.

## Tracks

1. Moving Back
2. Let Me Know/Cry
3. Kodoku Na Fruits (Vocal Version)
4. Hanasana
5. Kodoku Na Fruits

An instrumental and an a cappella version of the title track are also included as secret tracks 6 and 7.

## Credits

- Everything: Tobokegao

## About the making

Made in M8 Tracker. The title track later appeared on the album "TOBOX" [TBK-019] as "Kodoku Na Fruits".
