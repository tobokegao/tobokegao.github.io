## Overview
- Software: Caravan Boomer (a new shooting game for MSX2)
- Developer: N.I & BO DSGN
- Music: BGM played on PSG and MSX-MUSIC
- Status at the time: In development, announced for release in 2019 or 2020
- Tobokegao's part: Provided "黄泉で足踏み" (Yomi De Ashibumi), the BGM for stage 11 (Maak ported it to MSX2 for the game)

## Afterwards
- Friday, October 4, 2019: An early time-attack edition, "Caravan Boomer Caravan Edition!" (3.5-inch floppy disk), went on sale at 家電のケンちゃん in Tokyo.
- Saturday, January 25, 2020: An FM demo version of the track was included on "ESC TRAX 5th Anniversary".
- Monday, February 3, 2020: A Game Boy remake, "黄泉で足踏み (Caravan Boomer VGM GB TV Ver)", was posted.
- August 2020: The full version was being shipped to preorder buyers; by August 19, 60% of the preorders had been shipped.
- Saturday, March 13, 2021: SuperSweep (SweepRecord) released the soundtrack [SRIN-1177], with "黄泉で足踏み (Stage 11)" as track 12 and its Another Ver. as track 25 (sold early at the Tokyo Game Music Show on March 6).
- Friday, August 6, 2021: "黄泉で足踏み (Vocal Version)", with vocals, was released.
