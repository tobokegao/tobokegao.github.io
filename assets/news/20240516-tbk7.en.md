## Looking back
The night was planned with no DJ sets: every performer would play a machine live set using a Game Boy. To let people experience it in the room, there was no stream. This volume also tried taking advance reservations through a ticket site for the first time. Afterwards, Tobokegao posted the setlist and asked anyone who had filmed Tobokegao's set to send the video.
