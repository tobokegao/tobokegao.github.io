## Overview
- Release date: Monday, September 10, 2018 (SoundCloud)
- Release: Shady Monk's "Splitting Hairs (feat. tobokegao)"
- Tobokegao's part: Some of the melody, played on a Game Boy

## Afterwards
- September 17, 2018: Tobokegao's remix, "SHADY MONK - Splitting Hairs (feat. tobokegao) (tobokegao LSDj Remix)", went up on SoundCloud and YouTube
- April 17, 2019: The single "Splitting Hairs (feat. とぼけがお)" came out on streaming services

## About the making
Collaborating with someone who does not use LSDj turned out to be hard, so for the remix Tobokegao sampled Shady Monk's stems (the separate parts) into LSDj to blend them in.
