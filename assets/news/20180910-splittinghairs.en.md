## Overview
- Release date: Monday, September 10, 2018 (SoundCloud)
- Release: Shady Monk's "Splitting Hairs (feat. tobokegao)"
- Tobokegao's part: Game Boy (LSDj) remix

## Afterwards
- September 17, 2018: The same remix was also released on Tobokegao's own SoundCloud and YouTube as "SHADY MONK - Splitting Hairs (feat. tobokegao) (tobokegao LSDj Remix)"
- April 17, 2019: The single "Splitting Hairs (feat. とぼけがお)" was released on streaming platforms

## About the making
Collaborating with an artist who doesn't use LSDj was challenging, so Tobokegao imported Shady Monk's stems (the individual audio tracks) into LSDj. The Game Boy's wave channel chops up the original track with a stutter effect, while the pulse and noise channels layer in more parts.
