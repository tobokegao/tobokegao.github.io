## Overview
- Date: Wednesday, May 4, 2022, from 18:00 (15 minutes per act)
- Venue: Maple Leaf Club, Toyohira Ward, Sapporo
- Organizer: Maple Leaf Club
- Event: A two-day 3rd anniversary event. May 4 was the day for free game play and live game music
- Tobokegao's part: The 18:00 slot "とぼけがお(chiptune)" in the game music part, playing guitar, vocals, Game Boy, and M8 Tracker

## Lineup (May 4, game music part)
- 15:00– あまやん
- 16:00– GB's with T / びろレモ
- 17:00– ヘパリーゼ阿修羅の章
- 18:00– とぼけがお(chiptune)

## Looking back
This was Tobokegao's first offline show in about five years. With the Game Boy and M8 Tracker plugged into the same mixer, the volume gap was large, and the LSDj sound came out very quiet. The cover played at the show was later recorded and released on TBKgao on May 6 as the EP "Scythe - Sonna Hi Mo Aru Yone (Tobokegao Vocal Cover)".
