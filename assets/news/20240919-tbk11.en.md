## Overview
- Date: Thursday, September 19, 2024, 19:00–22:00
- Venue: PLASTIC THEATER, Sapporo
- Theme: M8 Tracker and chiptune (chiptune, techno, dubstep and more)
- Admission: 2,500 yen in advance / 3,000 yen at the door

- Tobokegao (LIVE): singing and playing guitar over the M8 and the Game Boy

## Looking back
This was an M8 Tracker night in Hokkaido, held as a side event of M8 FEST. ADOL, sdhizumi and uraboroshi came up from the Kanto area. Tobokegao opened the night, with plans to show the tobocaster in a live set for the first time.
