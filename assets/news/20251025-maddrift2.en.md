## Overview
- Date: Saturday, October 25 and Sunday, October 26, 2025 (Tobokegao played on the 25th)
- Venue: Musashino Art University's festival (a basement space on campus)
- Theme: An event that keeps otoMADs mixing back to back (no stream)
- Tobokegao's part: otoMAD mix (20 minutes)

## Looking back
As the number of performers grew, the plan changed to a 20-minute otoMAD mix with a free hand. On the day, the first half focused on overseas YTPMVs, and the second half went wherever Tobokegao liked. The biggest reaction came with the "there's a monkey in my bathroom" meme. The next day, the mix was posted on YouTube and Niconico.
