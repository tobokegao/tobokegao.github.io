## Overview
- Started: Saturday, October 24, 2020
- Project: "おとのかたみち", a shared note by 赤帯 (Akaobi) and Tobokegao
- Concept: The two each bought 10,000 yen worth of used CDs, sent them to each other, and reviewed the CDs they received
- Count: 赤帯 chose 20 and Tobokegao chose 27, for 47 works in total
- Cover: スレンテンノイ (Sleng Teng Neu!)

## How it worked
Tobokegao introduced the CDs from 赤帯 one by one in replies on X, and wrote the finer details, such as impressions of each track and the players, on note. Not all the CDs have been reviewed yet, and the project is still going.
