## Overview
- Date: Monday, October 10, 2016
- Work: "A.M.O.C.", a vertical shoot 'em up made for GBJAM 5
- Release: itch.io (Windows, name your own price)
- Code and art: IkaRisu (VECTRAX)
- Music: Tobokegao, PainPerdu8bit, azuria_sky, and IkaRisu (as listed on the itch.io page)
- Tobokegao's part: Co-wrote the stage 1 music with IkaRisu (mainly the bass and the B section)

## Looking back
The game brought together several LSDj musicians. All the music and sound effects were made with LSDj running on Game Boy hardware. It was an early prototype made as a self-imposed 48-hour challenge, with plans to keep developing it after the deadline.
