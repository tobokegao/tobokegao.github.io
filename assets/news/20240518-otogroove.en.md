## Looking back
Tobokegao aimed for a 15-minute mix that fits a huge number of clips together like a puzzle, and asked followers for otoMAD material to slip in. For a while work looked likely to keep Tobokegao away, but a day off came through and the set was played in person. While the mix was screened, Tobokegao got up on the Venue 1 stage and moved along with the video, and closed with a collaboration by めがー and みんみん. The mix went up on Niconico the next day, followed by a version that adds Venue 2's audio to Venue 1's video and sound, on both Niconico and YouTube.
