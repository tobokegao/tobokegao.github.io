## About the making
Everyone used the same spoken lines and the same song, reshaping the rhythm of the lines to fit the music. Tobokegao fitted the lines in Melodyne and aimed for a swinging timing. Stretching the words out as far as possible was fun, so that went in too. The plugins used are listed in the note article the organizer published.
