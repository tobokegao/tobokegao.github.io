## Overview
- Released at: Comic Market 93 (Friday, December 29, 2017, East Y11a)
- Project: 地獄でなぜサグい
- Title: reversible chance operation
- Format: CD (oil was splashed at random on each disc, so every copy sounds different and is one of a kind; a wet tissue is included, and wiping off the oil lets you hear the original sound)
- Tobokegao's part: Track 2, "Gameboy Noise Jam 002 (Circuit Bent)"

## About the making
A noise track made on an original Game Boy circuit-bent by Taniura (@banzainuigurumi, synth player in the band 世紀マ3), full of glitchy sounds like cicadas and a construction site. Regular noise meets the randomness of an oiled CD. The CD is not on Bandcamp.
