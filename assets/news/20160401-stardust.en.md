## Overview
- Release date: Friday, April 1, 2016
- Label: MADMILKY RECORDS
- Release: Skybox's "Stardust EP" (6 tracks; digital and CD)
- Track: 2. "æther (feat. Tobokegao)"
- Track 6 is a remix of this song by Slime Girls, "æther (Slime Girls Tropicana Slip N Slide Remix)."
- Skybox was earlier known as Shoujo Kiss, and later as mikey303.

## About the making
In 2015, Skybox (then Shoujo Kiss) invited Tobokegao to make a song together, and the two made it in LSDj. They took turns adding melodies, and it took more than half a year to finish. Tobokegao was very surprised to hear later that it would go on an EP from MADMILKY RECORDS. Tobokegao posted that having Slime Girls remix it felt like a dream. Tobokegao often uses a steel-drum-like sound made in LSDj as a bell, and wrote of never expecting Slime Girls to turn the song into a tropical remix full of steel drums.

## Afterwards
- In September 2018, when a track from this EP played during "#SST2018," Tobokegao posted about wanting to brag about having made it together.
- In May 2020, the track was also included on TBKgao's collaboration collection "Collab Works 01" as "æther (feat. とぼけがお)" and released on streaming services.
- In September 2024, in a post introducing mikey303, who was also playing at m8 fest, Tobokegao pointed to this track as a song the two had made together in LSDj.
