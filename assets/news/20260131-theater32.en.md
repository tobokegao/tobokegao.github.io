## Overview
- Date: Saturday, January 31, 2026, doors at 20:00 (Tobokegao's set started at 20:40)
- Venue: PLASTIC THEATER, Sapporo
- Theme: PLASTIC THEATER's 32nd anniversary party (the venue turned 32 on New Year's Eve 2025)
- Admission: 2,000 yen (two drinks included)
- Tobokegao's part: Live set (billed as "とぼけがお (To Be Kontinued)")

## Looking back
The anniversary party brought together all of the venue's regular parties, and Tobokegao played live in the To Be Kontinued slot. The day before, Tobokegao had said the plan was to program rhythms while playing guitar and singing. On the day of the show, the performance involved composing a song on the spot before singing it. The setup kept glitching until just before the show, and it glitched again during the set, but it made an interesting sound, so Tobokegao called it a win.
