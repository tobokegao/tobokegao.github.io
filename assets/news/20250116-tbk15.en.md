## Overview
- Date: Thursday, January 16, 2025, 19:00–22:00
- Venue: PLASTIC THEATER, Sapporo
- Theme: FM synthesis and chiptune
- Admission: 2,000 yen in advance / 2,500 yen at the door
- Flyer: Tobokegao

## Lineup
- ヨナオケイシ (LIVE): known for FM music in X68000 and PC games, the あすか120% series and この世の果てで恋を唄う少女YU-NO (PS4/Vita version).
- 新田忠弘 (LIVE): known for music for the PC-88 Xak series and シェンムー, and for playing the shakuhachi.
- Tobokegao (LIVE): planned to explore FM sounds to the fullest on the M8 Tracker, but switched to a more live-oriented DJ set on the day.
- 玉川とし (DJ): organizer of the VRChat chiptune event バーチャルチップチューンナイト.
- TERA (DJ): a chipstep digger met through VRChat, on the To Be Kontinued lineup for the third time.

## Looking back
The night started right on time. ヨナオケイシ and 新田忠弘 both had merch tables. Hearing shakuhachi and FM sound in the theater was deeply satisfying, and 玉川とし's chiptune hardcore and ヨナオケイシ's chord choices were real highlights.
