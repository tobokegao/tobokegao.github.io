## Looking back
Gathering artists Tobokegao loves made for a night with wildly mixed genres. The EP was not finished in time, so Tobokegao played the many demos made along the way instead. It was a joy to hear so many distorted kicks, and they went surprisingly well with chiptune-like sounds.
