## Overview
- Date: Thursday, July 27, 2023, doors 18:30 / start 19:00
- Venue: PLASTIC THEATER, Sapporo
- Theme: A birthday party aiming for a feel somewhere between chiptune and digital fusion
- Admission: 3,500 yen in advance / 4,500 yen at the door (a same-day discount for signing up on twipla); a streaming ticket was also sold
- Tobokegao's part: Organizer, plus the opening mix

## Lineup
- D.watt: Closed the night and wrapped the party up perfectly.
- golgi
- H B & VJ suleiman_1 (VR Set)
- 古町MOI: Also performed a cover of Tobokegao's "黄泉で足踏み."
- Key Blue: Mixed mod-flavored tracks with hard-hitting Game Boy tracks.
- lawi0cir (LAUSBUB): A solo set by Rico of LAUSBUB.

- Tobokegao
- Food: ジュンタラカレー

## Looking back
To keep the entrance from getting crowded, tickets were sold as e-tickets checked with QR codes. The stream's archive stayed viewable until August 26. Hearing a cover of one of Tobokegao's own songs was a moving moment. Afterwards, Tobokegao said the night gave the courage to keep working hard at many things.
