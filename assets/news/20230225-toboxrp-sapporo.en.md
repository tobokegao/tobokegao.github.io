## Overview
- Date: Saturday, February 25, 2023, 15:00–21:00
- Venue: PLASTIC THEATER, Sapporo
- Organizer: Tobokegao (the release party for the third album, "TOBOX")
- Admission: 2,500 yen in advance, 3,500 yen at the door (the advance price applied to anyone who signed up on twipla)
- Stream: Yes (PLASTIC THEATER's Twitch)
- Tobokegao's part: DJ set (19:50–20:20)

## Performers
- DJ: さうすまうぅん, cvel, 嶽本サライ, 赤帯, Tobokegao, hally
- Live: tktc szk, シロシビンズ, 梅本佑利, Ca5, うみのひやけ
- Video screening: 豊平区民

嶽本サライ appeared by video for personal reasons. 60 Million Reserve, who had been scheduled to perform, cancelled.

## Looking back
The next day, Tobokegao posted "20230225 TOBOX Release Party In Sapporo" on SoundCloud, a mix recreating the DJ set. On February 28, the T-shirts left over from the event went on sale on Bandcamp, limited to seven.
