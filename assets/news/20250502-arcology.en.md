## About the release

An arcology is an ideal city contained within one massive vertical structure. The album tells the story of a futuristic tower-city built in Tokyo Bay, inspired by megastructure proposals such as X-Seed 4000 and the Tokyo Tower of Babel. Each track stands for a different level of the tower.

- Foundation Stone: the monolithic base and the industrial sector
- Sunken Dreams: the struggles, expectations and disappointments of people living in the residential biodome
- Neon Games: the opulent entertainment level
- Emotion Algorithm: the high-tech research sector
- Exosphere: a ride in the space elevator, past secret military zones to the Kármán line
- Cosmos: a look down from above that ends the journey

Three tracks came out ahead of the album as singles: "Arcology" [TBK-029], "Sunken Dreams" [TBK-034] and "Cosmos" [TBK-036].
