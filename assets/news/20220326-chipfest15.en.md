## Overview

- Date: Saturday, March 26, 2022, from 7 p.m. UK time (4 a.m. on Sunday, March 27 in Japan)
- Place: streamed on Twitch
- Tobokegao's part: ten songs sung in 30 minutes, recorded in a rented studio and run through a cassette MTR.

It was Tobokegao's first live show in a while, and Tobokegao finally got to join CHIPFEST CYBERSPACE, now at its 15th edition.
