## Overview
- Released at: Comic Market 92, day 1 (Friday, August 11, 2017, East Hall 7, block "の" 13a)
- Circle: ああ…翡翠茶漬け…
- Title: Chippin' Party! #02 (the second pico-pico compilation) [AHC-003]
- Format: CD (1,000 yen, 13 tracks)
- Tobokegao's part: Track 3, "BMX Matatabi Grand Prix Theme BGM (2 Loop Mix)" (Game Boy, LSDj)
- Illustration and design: むらたむうた

## Afterwards
On Monday, November 26, 2018, the track was posted on SoundCloud. It shows a little influence from Motocross Maniacs 2 and Pokémon. For the video recreation of the Square Sounds Tokyo 2017 live set (released in September 2018), cover artist むらたむうた created the visuals for this track.
