## About the making

COVID-19 brought thoughts of death and of living much closer. So the album was made on a Game Boy around one concept: getting away from the desire to live and the desire to die.

For the artwork, Tobokegao asked a favorite pixel artist, toyoya, to draw two rooms, a bright one and a dark one, in a single picture. Under COVID-19, the more people there are in a room, the more you have to be aware of death when moving from room to room, and Tobokegao wanted to stay immersed in music even then. The two rooms were also meant to express the feeling of being alive and the feeling of being dead.

The album was made while moving back and forth between a sunny room and a not-so-sunny room at home, so the artwork and the title felt like a good fit.

It comes with LSDj 8.9.6 .sav data as a bonus.
