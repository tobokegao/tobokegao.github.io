## About the release

The release announcement introduced MLTEK as "the midi scientist", who set out on a long journey some years ago, has released four records, and made most of the work in a personal MIDI laboratory somewhere in Arizona.

TBKgao later released "About Image Info" [TBK-016] and "MIDI Science Intellectual Property" [TBK-023] as well.
