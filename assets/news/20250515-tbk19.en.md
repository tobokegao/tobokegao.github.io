## Looking back
It was a truly fun night. At Tobokegao's request, 岡村靖幸's "カルアミルク" was also played. The DJ-mix played while doors were open turned into something like lesser-known Shibuya-kei, and was later shared on SoundCloud as "To Be Kontinued Vol.19 90s J-POP mix".
