## Looking back
There was no stream for this volume. Tobokegao played VOCALOID songs by wwhhooami and doctorn0gloff, both makers of chiptune and Digital Fusion, and found it fun that in both songs the VOCALOID sings scat. フロクロ played "歯みがきガムのうた" in their set, which reminded Tobokegao how much the song had been a favorite. Tobokegao's DJ mix went up on SoundCloud as "chiptune/digital fusion VOCALOID mix".
