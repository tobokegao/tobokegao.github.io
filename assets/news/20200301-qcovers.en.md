## Overview
- Released: Sunday, March 1, 2020
- Venue: M3-2020 Spring (Tokyo Ryutsu Center, Hall 1, O-16b)
- Circle: Q-records
- Title: Choro Q arrangement compilation "Q-Covers" (18 arrangers, 18 tracks)
- Price: 1,000 yen
- Tobokegao's part: Track 4, "Middle Size Circuit Theme (GB LSDj Short Loop Ver)"
- Original: the Middle / Long Circuit theme from Choro Q2

## About the making
Tobokegao imagined how the Middle / Long Circuit music from Choro Q2 would sound as the soundtrack of a Game Boy version. The odd time signatures were hard work.

## Afterwards
The compilation was also sold at M3 on Sunday, October 25, 2020, and sold out.
