## Overview
- Release date: Wednesday, July 20, 2016
- Label: Pterodactyl Squad (PTE063)
- Release: "Weezer - The Second 8-bit Album", a second tribute to Weezer with 14 songs made on sound chips from old game consoles (digital)
- Track: 11. "Homely Girl"
- Artwork: David Mauro
- Mastering: HarleyLikesMusic

## About the making
Tobokegao was the only Japanese participant, so Tobokegao covered "HOMELY GIRL" by Scott & Rivers, a unit in which Weezer's singer sings in Japanese, on the Game Boy.
