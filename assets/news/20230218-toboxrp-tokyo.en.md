## Overview
- Date: Saturday, February 18, 2023, 13:00–18:00
- Venue: Koenji Fourth Floor Ⅱ, Koenji
- Organizer: Tobokegao (the release party for the third album, "TOBOX")
- Admission: 2,500 yen in advance, 3,500 yen at the door (the advance price applied to anyone who signed up on twipla)
- Stream: Yes (Tobokegao's Twitch)
- Tobokegao's part: DJ set (from 13:00) and live set (from 16:30)

## Performers
- DJ: Tobokegao, AVGN Crew, hosotake, konamiscc
- Live: ヤギシロ, Rei8bit, ツポールヌ, sdhizumi, Tobokegao, 葛飾出身
