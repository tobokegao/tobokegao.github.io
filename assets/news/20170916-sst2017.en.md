## Overview
- Date: Saturday, September 16, 2017
- Venue: Koenji HIGH
- Event: Square Sounds Tokyo 2017
- Tobokegao's part: Live set (Game Boy Advance SP, LSDj, 30 minutes)

## Looking back
Tobokegao chose songs that felt right to play live, so only two were originals, and the set became something like a mashup of 20 songs. It flowed from city pop through minimal techno, C64 big beat, trap and video game music to J-pop. Several songs were packed into one data file and the tones were changed on the spot (five data files in all).

## Afterwards
On Friday, September 14, 2018, a mix recreating this set, "Square Sounds Tokyo 2017 tobokegao Mix", was released free on SoundCloud. A version accompanied by video was released via AMDISCS.
