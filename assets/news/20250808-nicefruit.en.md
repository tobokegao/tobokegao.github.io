## About the release

A collection of warm and gentle songs, the kind you want to hear on a tropical night. Okumura of henrytennis, whom Tobokegao had known for some time, brought the idea, and the album came out on TBKgao. On release day, the music site Mikiki also ran it as news.

The following year its songs were remade with MIDI sounds and synthesized vocals on the compilation "Nice Fruit - MellMell x Pure MIDI x Voice Synthesis Arrange Compilation" [TBK-040].
