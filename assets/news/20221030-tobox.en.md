## Overview

- Release date: October 30, 2022
- Format: CD-R, digital (Bandcamp) and streaming services
- Label: TBKgao [TBK-019]
- Price: the CD-R on Bandcamp was $18 plus shipping; at M3 Autumn 2022 it was 2,000 yen, or more if you liked.

## About the making

Twelve songs built mainly on M8 Tracker, Game Boy and vocals, in a mix of chiptune, digital fusion and power pop, made with J-POP in mind. The CD-R comes in a paper sleeve with a perspective trick that makes the eyes seem to follow you. The song titles are in Japanese on the CD-R only, and in English everywhere else.

## Looking back

It sold far better than expected at M3, so 100 more CD-Rs were printed. The second edition has a slightly different mix and disc design, and Bandcamp orders were set to ship in early December. The CD-R was also sold through Melon Books online and at LIKE A DREAMING GIRL, a cassette shop inside haircut VOX in Hokkaido.

Tobokegao was glad that more people picked it up on CD-R than as a download.
